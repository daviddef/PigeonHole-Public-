# How to get an app password

Pigeonhole signs in with an **app-specific password**, not your normal password. This is safer: you can revoke it at any time without changing your real password.

## iCloud Mail

1. Go to <https://account.apple.com> and sign in.
2. Open **Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords**.
3. Choose **Generate an app-specific password**, name it "Pigeonhole", and copy it (it looks like `abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop`).
4. In Pigeonhole, use your iCloud address (for example `name@icloud.com`) and paste that password.

## Google (Gmail)

1. Turn on **2-Step Verification** for your Google account if it isn't on yet.
2. Go to <https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords>.
3. Create an app password named "Pigeonhole" and copy the 16 letters.
4. In Pigeonhole, choose **Google (Gmail)**, use your Gmail address and paste the password.

If the page isn't available, your account may be managed by an organisation that disables app passwords: ask its administrator.

## Revoking

Delete the app password on the same page whenever you like. Pigeonhole will then fail to sign in until you make a new one.
