What it is for

Years of mail collect copies, look-alike folders and forgotten attachments. Pigeonhole's job is to give every message one tidy place, without risking any of it.

Keep the mail, lose the clutter

Find the same message saved twice, folders that mean the same thing ("Sent Items" and "Sent Messages"), and the big attachments using your storage.

Safe by design

Nothing is permanently deleted. Removed mail goes to Trash, every change is recorded, and most can be undone in one click.

Private by design

No account, no cloud service, no tracking. Your Mac talks only to your own mail provider. The one optional exception, Ask Claude in Sort Gmail, is off until you switch it on.

Simple to use

Three tabs and a card for each job. You see what it found and why before anything changes.

Features

Works with iCloud Mail and Google (Gmail) over IMAP, and can look through the mail already stored on your Mac.

How it works

Three steps. Nothing changes until you say so.

  1. ConnectSign in with an app-specific password for iCloud or Google. You can have it remembered in the Mac keychain, or not.
  2. ScanPigeonhole reads message headers only. It does not change or mark anything as read. You watch progress folder by folder.
  3. Review and applyYou see what it found and why, choose what to keep, and apply. Each move is checked against the server first, and the counts are re-read afterwards.

Safety and privacy

Pigeonhole asks for an app password, so here is plainly what it does with it. The trust page explains how to check each claim yourself.

QuestionAnswer
Does my mail leave my Mac?Only to your own mail provider (and the second account if you use Move mail). There is no Pigeonhole server. The one optional exception is Ask Claude in Sort Gmail, off by default: it sends sender, subject and a short text snippet to Anthropic using your own API key.
Where is my password kept?In the macOS Keychain, and only if you tick Remember. Never in a file or log.
Is anything sent for analytics or crash reports?No.
Can it permanently delete mail?No. Removed messages go to Trash, and it never empties your Trash.
Can I undo?Yes. History lists every change, and most can be undone with one click.
Is the app trustworthy to install?It is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, with the hardened runtime on.

Questions

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 12 (Monterey) or later, on Apple silicon or Intel, and an iCloud or Gmail account. Nothing else needs installing: Python is included.

What is an app-specific password?

A separate password that lets one app use your mail without your main password. You create it in your Apple or Google account and can revoke it at any time. Step-by-step guide.

Is it open source?

No. Pigeonhole is closed source, with a free trial of 500 changed messages and then a one-time US$9.99 licence. The documentation is public so you can read what it does, and the trust page shows how to verify its behaviour with a network monitor and Apple's notarisation check.

Does it work with Outlook or other providers?

Not yet. Today it supports iCloud Mail and Google (Gmail), and reads Apple Mail's local files.

Something went wrong. Where do I look?

See troubleshooting, or open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post passwords or mail contents.
